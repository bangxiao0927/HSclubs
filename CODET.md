## User Preferences

- All user-facing copy should remain in English across the project.
- The long-term product direction is a single-school clubs platform that other schools can adopt by copying the pattern.
- The first school deployment is MVHS (Mountain View High School) on `mvhs.hsclubs.net`; the guiding page is the apex `hsclubs.net`. Keep school identity defaults consistent with that unless the operator says otherwise.
- After completing a PR (merging/resolving conflicts), always open a follow-up commit to self-review the changes and verify PR status checks pass before marking the PR as ready.
